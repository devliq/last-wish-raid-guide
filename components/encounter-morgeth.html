Mechanics
Morgeth, the Spirekeeper
1
Clear the Arena
Defeat all Taken enemies until a Servitor spawns. Kill it to start the orb phase. Call out orb locations: "Left" or "Right".
2
Orb Collection
Designate 1-2 players as Carriers. They collect Taken Strength orbs (max 2). NEVER pick up a 3rd orb—it's instant death. Call your stack count constantly.
3
DPS Phase
When Morgeth has 2 stacks of Taken Strength, he becomes vulnerable. All Carriers dump orbs simultaneously. Stack in Well and burn.
4
Repeat Until Dead
Morgeth typically takes 2-3 phases. After DPS, he retreats and new orbs spawn. Repeat until dead. Watch for Enrage timer (~7 minutes).
💥
DPS Strategy
Optimal damage phase
🔥
Synchronized DPS
All Carriers dump orbs at exactly 2 stacks. This maximizes DPS time. Well of Radiance + weapon buffs essential.
🛡️
Carrier Survival
Carriers need high Health stat. Use Taken Barrier raid mod. Never stand still with 2 orbs—keep moving.
