Mechanics
Shuro Chi, the Corrupted
1
Tic-Tac-Toe Puzzle
Three symbols appear on plates around the room. One player (Runner) navigates the three rooms (Stairs, Trees, Rocks) to match all 9 symbols before the song timer expires.
2
Shoot the Eye
After completing a room's puzzle, shoot the Eye of Riven that spawns. All players must damage it—not just the Runner. Use rapid-fire weapons.
3
DPS Phase
After all 3 rooms are cleared, Shuro Chi becomes vulnerable. Stack in Well of Radiance and burn. Watch for her tracking Arc attack—keep moving.
4
Repeat or One-Phase
Strong DPS can one-phase. Otherwise, repeat puzzle + DPS until dead. Remember: song timer = ~60 seconds per puzzle.

DPS Strategy
Rotation patterns
🔥
One-Phase DPS
Well of Radiance required. All stack tight. Use Golden Gun + Swords. Thundercrash Titan helps. 3× Solar Surge mods recommended.
🏃
Runner Priority
Runner should NOT delay DPS. Fast movement builds essential. Use Transversive Steps or Dragon's Shadow for maximum speed.
