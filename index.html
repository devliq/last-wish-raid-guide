⚔️
Mechanics
Kalli, the Corrupted
1
Match the 6 Plates
Six symbols float around the center arena. Match each plate's symbol to one of the six. Assign one player per matching plate. Learn your symbol before starting - the symbols rotate weekly.
2
Stand on Plates + Kill Knights
All players stand on their assigned plate. A Taken Knight spawns near each — kill all six to begin the DPS phase on Kalli. Coordinate weapon element for Harmonic Splicer catalysts if needed.
3
DPS Phase
Move to center and burn Kalli. When she readies her Ontological Weapon, six doors open around the room — all players must enter a door or wipe. Kalli has 4 health bars - each door phase is one bar.
4
Repeat Until Dead
Kalli is typically one-phased with good DPS. Equip 3 Surge mods + 3 Reserve mods matching your heavy weapon element. Well of Radiance for 25% damage buff.
💥
DPS Strategy
Rotation patterns for optimal damage
🔥
One-Phase Setup
Well of Radiance + Lucent Finish buff. All players stack tight. Golden Gun + Swords for burst. Shotguns if close range. Coordinate Heavy weapon timing.
🔄
Multi-Phase Rotation
Each door is a separate phase. Reset position after each. Use Thundercrash for one door, then swords for remaining. Keep moving between doors.

Mechanics
Shuro Chi, the Corrupted
1
3 Floors, 6 DPS Phases
Shuro Chi's health is divided into 6 segments. Two DPS phases per floor across three floors. Break each health bar segment. Each floor has 3 rooms: Stairs (left), Trees (middle), Rocks (right).
2
Tic-Tac-Toe Plate Puzzle
Three rooms per floor, each has a 3×3 grid of symbols. Runners stand on the three plates matching the center device's symbols. Call middle symbol first, then remaining two in any order.
3
Kill Eye of Riven + DPS
After puzzle, the Eye of Riven spawns. Kill it and collect the Taken Essence. Use the grenade ability to trigger DPS phase. Don't let the song timer expire - wipe occurs shortly after.
4
Penumbral vs Antumbral
Eye of Riven drops either Penumbral or Antumbral Taken Essence. Note which one — it must be popped on the matching plate type. Penumbral = white sphere, Antumbral = dark sphere.
💥
DPS Strategy
Floor-by-floor rotation guide
🔥
Floor 1 Rotation
Stairs → Trees → Rocks. Runner calls "Floors: Stairs first, Trees second, Rocks third" to establish order. Focus on clearing adds efficiently.
🔥
Floor 2 Rotation
Rocks → Stairs → Trees. Reverse order. Same player order, different room assignments. Maintain communication about next room.
🔥
Floor 3 Rotation
Trees → Rocks → Stairs. Final rotation. All players should have weapons ready for DPS. Well of Radiance between phases if available.
👥

Stairs · Trees · Rocks layout

Runner

× 3
Gunner
One Gunner per room. Clear ads for the Runner. Pair with a Runner and defend them throughout each floor.
× 2
DPS Lead
Coordinates when to pop Taken Essence and call DPS phase. Usually Runners from last active room after escape.

Challenge: Which Witch — never get hit by Shuro Chi's long-range Arc attack.

Pro Tips
💡
Stick to Your Plate
Remember which plate you stood on — if you're off it in the tic-tac-toe phase, hop back on fast. Coordination wipes here more than anything else.
💡
Forced Room Mechanic
After solving, a force-field traps the Runner in their room. Other players call out which room is now open so the Runner knows which tunnel to take.
💡
Hidden Chest
First hidden chest is below the bridge just before this encounter. Find it for a chance at raid mods.

Mechanics
Morgeth, the Spirekeeper
1
Taken Strength Stacking
Taken Strength orbs spawn throughout the arena. You can hold a maximum of 2 at a time — grabbing a 3rd kills you instantly. Plan carefully. Orbs reset to 0 upon death.
2
Designated Carrier
Assign one player to pick up the FIRST Taken Strength and the LAST one. Their purpose is to never touch another until the final collection. This player needs high health/survivability.
3
Left Side Collection
After first Taken Strength is grabbed, carrier runs to the left side. Other players collect additional orbs, rotating when at max stacks. Callouts: "1 stack", "2 stacks", "dropping to 0".
4
Stagger & DPS
Collect enough Taken Strength to stagger Morgeth. During stagger, all players unload maximum DPS. Rinse and repeat until dead. Power creep allows one-phase with sufficient orbs.
💥
Orb Collection Strategy
Optimal path and coordination
🔄
Orb Path Priority
Carrier: First orb → Left ledge → Last orb. Collectors: Front orbs first, then back, always staying at or below 2 stacks. Call "handoff ready" when approaching carrier.
🔥
Stagger Damage
With enough orbs (12+), Morgeth staggers easily. All players dump heavy weapons. Supers: Shadowshot for debuff, Well for sustained DPS, Fists of Havoc for burst.
👥
Role Assignments
× 1
First/Last Carrier
Picks up first Taken Strength and reserves the last. Must not touch any other orb between these two events. Critical role.
× 3–4
Orb Collectors
Collect Taken Strength orbs, staying at or below 2 stacks. Hand off to the next player when maxed. Call your stack count.
× 1
Well / Support
Warlock drops Well during DPS windows. Also calls stagger timing so team knows when to stack and burst.

Challenge: Forever Flight — do NOT kill any Ogres that spawn during this encounter.

✅
Pre-Encounter Checklist
  • Assign First/Last Carrier role before starting
  • All players know the 2-stack max rule (3 = instant death)
  • Good ad-clear weapon equipped (Morgeth spawns many ads)
  • For challenge: agree NOT to shoot Ogres
  • Hidden chest location scouted (across large gap, before this encounter)
🔮
Key Notes
⚠️
3 Stacks = Instant Wipe
The most common cause of death here. Call out your current stack count constantly. If you see 2 stacks on yourself, move away from orbs immediately.
💡
Power Creep Makes This Easy
Morgeth can usually be one-phased in the current meta. Focus on collecting enough orbs to get one stagger and dump everything into the boss.

Mechanics
Open the Vault
1
Three Rooms, Three Locations
The Vault has three locations: Stairs (left), Trees (middle), and Rocks (right). Same layout as Shuro Chi. Assign teams of two to each room. Only ONE room is open at a time.
2
Identify Open Room
A Taken wall blocks 2 of the 3 rooms. One room is always open. The player whose room is open enters, kills the Eye of Riven, and takes the Essence. Non-open runners stay at room entrances.
3
Force Field Rotation
After taking the Essence, a force field traps the Runner. Others tell them which room is now open. Runner takes the side tunnel to escape to the open room. Call: "Go to [room name]".
4
Pop on Matching Plate × 2
Repeat the cycle twice — collect two Essences total, popping each on its matching plate to open the vault. Then DPS the boss. Penumbral/Energy vs Antumbral/Entropic plates.
💥

Post-vault DPS phase

🔥
Orb Phase DPS
Orbs spawn - collect them and dunk to break shield. All players should have Heavy ready. Well of Radiance during orb collection for safety. Focus fire on boss between phases.

Mechanics
Riven of a Thousand Voices
1
Split Into Two Rooms
Six players split into two rooms of three. Each room has Riven appearing — look for her eyes. There's a hidden starting room — run right from spawn, jump down 2 floors to find it.
2
Shoot the Eyes
Riven shows 2 eyes per stage, then more. Each guardian in the room memorizes which eyes they hit. For the Challenge: never shoot the same eye twice. 4 stages total.
3
DPS Phase — Mouth
When Riven opens her mouth, unload all DPS into it. She has 4 stages of eye reveals. Final stage has a black ball in her mouth — that's your main damage window. Black ball = max damage.
4
Queenswalk — Carry the Heart
After Riven dies: the Fates Chosen carries the Heart with a countdown timer. Call the last 10 seconds. When teleported to Eternity, non-carriers pick up 1 blight ASAP to extend timer.
💥
Eye Strategy & DPS Windows
Stage-by-stage breakdown
1️⃣
Stage 1: 2 Eyes
2 players shoot 1 eye each. Call "Stage 1: A1, A3" (positions). Quick mouth damage. Move back immediately after shooting.
2️⃣
Stage 2: 3 Eyes
3 players needed. All different from Stage 1. Mouth open wider - more damage. Track already-shot eyes carefully.
3️⃣
Stage 3: 4 Eyes
All 3 players shoot, leaving 1 eye. Mouth open very wide. This is the highest damage window before final stage.
🏁
Stage 4: Black Ball
Mouth has black ball - MAX damage window. All players dump everything. Swords, supers, heavies. Burn the heart immediately after.
👥
Role Assignments
× 3
Room A Team
Three guardians in left Riven room. Each memorizes which eyes they shoot across all 4 stages. Do not re-shoot your eyes for the challenge.
× 3
Room B Team
Three guardians in right Riven room. Same rules. Communicate with Room A team via callouts — Riven's rooms are mirrored.
× 1
Blight Keeper
During Queenswalk, responsible for collecting the final blight to extend timer. Designated first teleported player. Most important Queenswalk role.
× 1
Heart Carrier
Fates Chosen — carries Riven's Heart. Calls timer ("10 seconds!"). Move with the group toward dunk point. Everyone protects the carrier.

Challenge: Strength of Memory — never shoot the same Riven eye more than once.

  • Assign Room A and Room B teams (3 per room)
  • Designate Blight Keeper for Queenswalk
  • Best DPS loadout equipped — swords + heavy weapons
  • Everyone knows the hidden starting room path
  • Plan first teleport order for Queenswalk
  • Challenges active? Note eyes you shoot — no repeat shots

Advanced Guide

Strategy & Optimization

Advanced mechanics, positioning guides, callout systems, and common mistake avoidance.

📢
Callout Systems
Standardized communication across all encounters

Ahamkara Symbol Callouts

All symbols are called as "animal + direction". Example: "Dragon Left" or "Snake Up". The 4 animals are Bird, Snake, Fish, Dragon. Directions are Up, Left, Down, Right relative to the symbol facing.

Kalli / Vault Plates

Caller: "Bird Up" to indicate the plate with Bird facing Up. Each player confirms "Copy" when they reach their plate.

Shuro Chi Puzzles

Runner calls middle symbol first: "Dragon Left center, Dragon Right left, Snake Down right". Other plates called in any order.

Morgeth Team Comms

"Orbs Left/Right" for orb locations. "Carrier stacked" when 2x Taken Strength confirmed. "Ready for orb" for handoffs.

Vault Room Calls

"Stairs is open" or "Go Trees". Runner announces "I'm trapped" during force field rotation. "DPS room" after second solve.

Riven Room Coordination

Room A: Left room when splitting. Call eyes as "A1 Top Left" or "A2 Bottom Right".

Room B: Right room. Mirror positions to Room A. "B1 Top Right" corresponds to "A1 Top Left".

Queenswalk: "10 seconds" callout. "Blight up" when blights spawn in Eternity. "Teleport X" for teleport order.

⚠️
Common Mistakes & Fixes
Wipe prevention guide
❌
Kalli: Wrong Door Entry
Problem: Players enter wrong door or miss door entry. Fix: Identify your nearest door BEFORE DPS starts. Pre-designate Door 1-6. All players call "doors!" when Kalli readies weapon.
❌
Shuro Chi: Song Timer Expiry
Problem: Running out of time during tic-tac-toe puzzle. Fix: The song timer is ~60 seconds. When music stops, wipe soon after. Kill Eye immediately after puzzle solve — don't wait.
❌
Morgeth: 3 Stacks Death
Problem: Dying from picking up a 3rd Taken Strength orb. Fix: Call your stack count constantly. "At 1, going for 2" or "Dropping to 0, need 1 more." Carrier must stay at 0 stacks until final collection.
❌
Vault: Wrong Essence Type
Problem: Popping Antumbral on Penumbral plate (or vice versa). Fix: Eye always drops the same type you need. Penumbral = white sphere, Antumbral = dark sphere. Note which type before popping.
❌
Riven: Repeated Eye Shots
Problem: Shooting the same eye twice on Strength of Memory challenge. Fix: Each player tracks their own eyes. Use a numbered callout system: "Stage 1: A1, A3, B2, B4" for eye positions.
❌
Queenswalk: Blight Timing
Problem: Timer expires before reaching dunk point. Fix: Blight Keeper picks up LAST blight in Eternity. Non-carriers grab blights immediately on teleport. Stay close to carrier for protection.

Positioning Guides
Optimal positioning for each encounter

Kalli - Door Positions

Safe Door Strategy: Stand near the center after DPS. When doors open, run to the door closest to your plate position. Players 1-3 take left doors (1-3), Players 4-6 take right doors (4-6).

Shuro Chi - Floor Positioning

Stairs Room: Runner at back left plate, Gunner at door entrance covering Runner.
Trees Room: Runner center, Gunner covering flanks.
Rocks Room: Runner right side, Gunner at entrance.

Morgeth - Orb Collection Arc

Collection Flow: Start at center spawn, move to left side for first orb. Carrier stays on left ledge until final collection. Rotate clockwise through orbs to avoid collisions.

Vault - Room Escape Routes

Tunnel Navigation: Stairs connects to Trees (left tunnel). Trees connects to both Stairs (left) and Rocks (right). Rocks connects to Trees (left). Learn these before attempting.

Ahamkara Wishes

15 wishes activate at the wall before any encounter. Input sequences using the 4 animal symbols.

How to use: The Wish Wall is located before the first encounter. Look for the small lake on the left before entering Kalli's door. Follow the green grassy patches to find the room with a grid of circles. Shoot symbols to cycle them, then step on the center button to confirm. White flash = success, red flash = reset and try again. Source: idleanimation.com
