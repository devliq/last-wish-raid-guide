Mechanics
Riven of a Thousand Voices
1
Split into Two Rooms
3 players in Room A (left), 3 in Room B (right). Each room has 4 glowing eyes. Shoot the correct eye to start DPS.
2
Shoot Eyes in Order
Each player shoots ONE eye (Top Left, Top Right, Bottom Left, Bottom Right). All 8 eyes must be shot within ~10 seconds.
3
DPS Phase
Riven becomes vulnerable. All 6 players stack in Well of Radiance and burn. 3× Surge mods recommended. Strong DPS can one-phase.
4
Queenswalk (If Not One-Phased)
After DPS, one player becomes the Blight Carrier. They must travel through Eternity, dunk blights, and teleport back. Team protects them.
💥
DPS Strategy
Maximum damage
🔥
One-Phase DPS
Well of Radiance + Lucent Finish. All 6 stack tight. Use Swords + Golden Gun. Thundercrash Titan for burst. 3× Surge mods.
⚠️
Eye Callouts
Use "A1 Top Left" format. Each player tracks their own eyes for Strength of Memory challenge. Never shoot same eye twice.
